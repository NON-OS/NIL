# Brand voice

We write like a calm friend who knows money. People open Atlas when something matters to them, so every word should make the next step obvious.

## Principles

- **Plain words.** "Send", not "initiate a transfer".
- **Numbers over adjectives.** "Arrives in about 2 minutes", not "lightning fast".
- **Own the problem.** When something fails, say what happened and what to do next.

## Words we use

| Instead of | We say |
| --- | --- |
| Seed phrase | Backup phrase |
| Gas fee | Network fee |
| Insufficient funds | You need 0.002 more ETH for this |
| Transaction pending | On its way |

## Examples

> Your backup phrase is the only way back into this wallet if you lose your phone. Write it down on paper, in order.

> That address is on a different network. Check you picked Ethereum, not Polygon.
