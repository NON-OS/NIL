What NØNOS can and can’t see

  • Encrypted in your browser. Docs and private tasks, titles and comments included. NØNOS stores them and can’t read them.
  • Stored in plain form. Team tasks and their comments, files, goals and the member list. They live in your workspace’s own storage on NØNOS’s server. NØNOS doesn’t look at them, but as the host it could.
  • Nothing else. This page sets no cookies, loads nothing from other sites and runs no analytics.
