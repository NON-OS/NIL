# Views and finding things

The same tasks appear in several views. Pick the one that fits what you're doing.

## My work

My work is your home page. It lists the open tasks assigned to you in sections: New for you, Overdue, Today, Next 7 days, Later and No due date. "New for you" holds tasks someone else gave you in the last three days. The tiles at the top count what's due today, what's overdue and what you finished this week compared with last week.

The box at the top of **Assigned to me** adds a task for yourself. It understands the same shortcuts as the new task box, described in [Tasks](tasks.md).

## My Day

My Day, on the right of My work, is a short list of what you want to finish today. Click the sun icon on any task to add it. Only you can see this list. Tasks you didn't finish on earlier days show up underneath with a **Move to today** button.

## Inbox

The Inbox collects your notifications. You get one when:

- someone assigns you a task,
- someone mentions you in a comment,
- someone comments on a task you follow,
- a task you follow, created or are assigned changes status,
- someone changes the due date of a task assigned to you,
- a bounty is paid to you,
- an admin starts a vote,
- a reminder you set comes due,
- a task you have is due soon or due today.

Admins also hear when someone applies for a bounty on the public board.

The tabs show **All**, **Unread** or only **Mentions**. Click a notification to open its task, or click **Mark all read**. Read notifications are deleted after 90 days. The number in the browser tab's title is your unread count.

To hear about these when Nil is closed, turn on push notifications in **Settings → Notifications**, on each device you want them on. See [Time and reminders](time-and-reminders.md#push-notifications). **Desktop notifications**, on the same tab, show a system notification while Nil is open in a background tab. Neither one ever shows the text of a comment, since lock screens can show them.

## Board

The Board shows tasks in five columns: Backlog, To do, In progress, In review and Done. Drag a card to another column to change its status. On a touch screen, press and hold a card first. Canceled tasks aren't shown.

You can move cards with the keyboard too: press `Tab` until a card has focus, press `Space` to pick it up, use the arrow keys to move it and press `Space` again to drop it. `Esc` cancels.

## All tasks

All tasks is a list of every task you can see. Along the top:

- the filter box, which matches titles and task keys,
- filters for assignee, priority, label, project and due date (Overdue, Due today, Due this week or No due date),
- a **Group by** menu with Status, Assignee, Project, Priority, Due date and No grouping,
- a **Sort by** menu with Board order, Priority, Due date, Recently updated, Newest first and title order,
- a **Show completed** switch, which brings back done and canceled tasks.

Nil remembers your filters and grouping in this browser. **Clear** resets the filters.

Select rows to change many tasks at once, as described in [Tasks](tasks.md#change-many-tasks-at-once).

## Calendar

The Calendar puts tasks on a month grid by due date. Tasks with a due time show it and come first on their day, in time order. Drag a task to another day to move its due date; its time stays the same. The plus on a day creates a task due that day. A day shows four tasks; click "more" to see the rest. The arrows change the month.

### Your tasks in your own calendar app

In **Settings → Notifications**, under **Calendar feed**, click **Create calendar link**. Paste the link into Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook as a calendar to subscribe to, or click **Open in calendar app**. It lists the open tasks assigned to you that have a due date. Calendar apps check it every so often, usually within a few hours, so changes show up there late.

The link works without signing in, so anyone who has it sees those tasks: their names, dates and links. A private task shows only as "Private task", with no title, link or project. Keep the link to yourself. **Make a new link** stops the old one at once, and **Turn off** deletes it. It also stops working if you leave the workspace.

## Projects

Your projects are listed in the sidebar under **Projects**, each with its number of open tasks. Click the plus next to the heading to create one: give it a name, a short key (used in task keys and quick add), a colour, a description and a lead.

A project page shows its description, lead, overdue count and progress, then its tasks. Switch between **Board** and **List** at the top. The icons beside them share a read-only link (when share links are on), create a status deck and edit the project.

In **Settings → Projects** you can archive a project. Archived projects leave the sidebar but keep their tasks and history. Only admins can delete a project; its tasks move to "No project".

## Search and commands

Press `⌘K` on a Mac or `Ctrl+K` elsewhere, or `/`, or click **Search** at the top of the sidebar. Type to find:

- tasks by title or key, such as NON-42,
- projects and goals,
- any page, such as Bounties or Settings.

The same box runs actions: **Create a task**, **Create a status deck (.pptx)**, **Invite a teammate** for admins, and **Turn notes into tasks** when built-in AI is on. **Search help for** looks up what you typed in these help pages.

Use the arrow keys to move through the results, `Enter` to open one and `Esc` to close.

## Live updates

Changes appear for everyone at once, without reloading. The dot next to the Nil logo shows if you're connected; hover over it to see "Live" or "Reconnecting…". Teammates who are online appear under **Online now** in the sidebar. In a task panel you can see who else is looking at the same task and who is typing a comment.

Private tasks never show up in anyone else's live updates, and looking at one doesn't show you as viewing anything.
