# Goals, insights and decks

## Goals

A goal is an outcome your tasks add up to, like "Reach 1,000 paying customers". Goals are for members; guests don't see them.

To set one, open **Goals** and click **New goal**. Write the goal itself under **Outcome**, and a sentence or two of context under **Why it matters**. Then pick an owner, a status (On track, At risk, Off track or Achieved) and a target date.

If the goal has a number, such as monthly revenue, fill in **Track a number**: the **Metric** name and its **Start**, **Now** and **Target** values.

Link a task to a goal from the task's **Goal** field, or open the goal and click **Add task**.

Each goal card shows a progress bar. With a target number, the bar shows how far **Now** has moved from **Start** towards **Target**. Without one, it shows the share of linked tasks that are done. Canceled tasks don't count either way. Update **Now** whenever the number changes.

## Insights

**Insights** shows how the team is moving, counted from the tasks you can see:

- open tasks and how many are overdue,
- tasks finished in the last 7 days compared with the week before,
- the typical time from creating a task to finishing it (the median over the last 30 days),
- a chart of tasks finished per week over the last 12 weeks,
- open work per person, split by priority,
- progress per project and per goal,
- recent wins.

## Weekly brief

When built-in AI is on, Insights has a **Weekly brief** panel. Click **Write this week's brief** for a short summary of wins, risks and next week's focus. **Copy** puts it on your clipboard for your team chat. The brief stays in your browser tab and isn't saved. Private tasks are never sent to the AI for it. See [AI assistants](ai-assistants.md#built-in-ai) for what leaves your server.

## Status decks

A status deck is a PowerPoint file (.pptx) you can present or send. To start one, click **Create deck** on Insights or the presentation icon at the top of a project, or choose **Create a status deck (.pptx)** in the search box.

Choose what it covers (the whole workspace, one project or one goal) and the period (the last 7, 14 or 30 days). Turn on **Include my private tasks** if they belong in it. Then click **Download .pptx**.

The deck has a cover, an at-a-glance slide, what shipped, what is in progress, a goals chart you can edit in PowerPoint, risks, bounties and next steps, each with speaker notes. Your browser builds the file, so nothing leaves your device.

With built-in AI on, you can let the AI write the summary, highlights and speaker notes. Then the task titles, names and counts on the slides are sent to Anthropic for that one request. If you included your private tasks, their titles are among them.
