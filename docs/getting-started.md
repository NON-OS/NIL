# Getting started

Nil is a task app for teams that want their work kept private. You sign in with an Ethereum wallet instead of an email and password, and anything you mark as private is encrypted in your browser before it reaches the server.

## What you need

- A browser with an Ethereum wallet extension, such as MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet or the wallet built into Brave. On a phone, open Nil in the browser inside your wallet app.
- An invite link from someone in the workspace. You don't need one if you are setting up a new server, or if the workspace lets NOX holders join on their own.

You don't need tokens or ETH. Signing in means signing a short message, which is not a transaction and costs nothing.

## Your first sign-in

1. Open the invite link. The page says **Join** and the workspace name.
2. Type the name your team will see. A nickname is fine.
3. Click **Join with your wallet** and approve the signature in your wallet.

Next time, open the workspace address and click **Sign in with your wallet**. More detail is in [Signing in](signing-in.md).

If you are the first person on a new server, you see **Set up your workspace** instead. Enter a workspace name and your own name, then click **Create workspace with your wallet**. You become the owner. [People and roles](people-and-roles.md) explains how to invite the rest of your team.

## Find your way around

The sidebar on the left lists the main sections. Each has a two-key shortcut: press `G`, then the letter.

| Section | What it shows | Keys |
|---|---|---|
| My work | Tasks assigned to you, your My Day list and your reminders | `G` `H` |
| Inbox | Your notifications | `G` `I` |
| Board | Tasks in columns, from Backlog to Done | `G` `B` |
| All tasks | A list you can filter, group, sort and edit in bulk | `G` `L` |
| Calendar | Tasks on a month grid by due date | `G` `C` |
| Docs | End-to-end encrypted documents | `G` `D` |
| Goals | The outcomes your tasks add up to | `G` `G` |
| Insights | Charts of how the team is moving | `G` `S` |
| Time | Your private timesheet | `G` `M` |
| Bounties | Rewards on tasks and their payments | `G` `N` |
| Votes | Team decisions | `G` `V` |
| Proof of work | Your signed work receipts | `G` `P` |

Your projects are listed under the sections. Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar for **Settings** (`G` `T`), keyboard shortcuts and signing out. The question mark next to your name opens this help.

Guests see fewer sections: Docs, Goals and Votes are for members only.

To jump anywhere, press `⌘K` on a Mac or `Ctrl+K` elsewhere, or press `/`. Type part of a task title, a task key such as NON-42, a project or a page. Press `C` to create a task.

## Install Nil as an app

Nil is a progressive web app, so you can install it on a phone or computer and open it like any other app. Use your browser's install option. In Chrome and Edge it appears in the address bar; in Safari on an iPhone or iPad, tap Share and then Add to Home Screen.

The installed app keeps a copy of Nil's own files so it opens quickly. Your tasks and other data are never stored in that copy. They always come from your server.
