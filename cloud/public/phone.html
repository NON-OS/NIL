Use a wallet on your phone

Scan the code with the wallet app on your phone and keep working here. The sign-up message waits until you confirm it on your phone. The two talk through WalletConnect’s relay, encrypted end to end.

  1. Open your wallet appOn your phone: MetaMask, Rainbow, Trust Wallet or another wallet that works with WalletConnect.
  2. Scan this codeUse the scan button inside the wallet app. The phone’s camera app usually can’t open it.
  3. Approve the connectionYour wallet shows the name Nil and this site’s address. Then come back to this screen.
