# Nil documentation

Nil is the private task app for you and your team, made by the NØNOS foundation. These pages explain how it works, from your first sign-in to running your own server. The same pages are in the app under **Help**, and the search there covers all of them.

## Start here

- [Getting started](getting-started.md): what you need, your first sign-in and a tour of the screens.
- [Signing in](signing-in.md): your wallet as your account, a second wallet as a spare key, and what to do when sign-in fails.
- [People and roles](people-and-roles.md): owners, admins, members and guests, invites and the token-holder door.

## Everyday work

- [Tasks](tasks.md): creating tasks in one line, the task panel, comments, files and repeating tasks.
- [Views and finding things](views.md): My work, My Day, Inbox, Board, All tasks, Calendar, projects and search.
- [Time and reminders](time-and-reminders.md): the timer, your private timesheet and reminders.
- [Goals, insights and decks](goals-and-insights.md): goals with numbers, team charts and PowerPoint status decks.
- [Calls](calls.md): calls with sound, video and screens, encrypted end to end, and setting up the relay.
- [Keyboard shortcuts](keyboard-shortcuts.md): every key Nil listens for.

## Privacy and encryption

- [Private tasks](private-tasks.md): tasks only two people can read, encrypted in the browser.
- [Encrypted docs](encrypted-docs.md): team documents the server can't read.

- [Zero-state](zero-state.md): auto-erase, erasing everything and memory-only mode.
