Nil's code is free software under the [GNU AGPL v3 or later](LICENSE). The names and logos are not part of that licence. Section 7 of the AGPL lets a licensor withhold trademark rights, and the NØNOS foundation does.

"Nil" as the name of this software, "NØNOS", and the Nil and NØNOS logos belong to the NØNOS foundation.

## What you can do

- Say truthfully what your software is or works with: "a fork of Nil", "based on Nil", "works with Nil".
- Run an unmodified copy for your own team and call it Nil.
- Use the names in articles, reviews and talks about Nil.

## What needs written permission

- Calling a modified version, or a service that hosts Nil for other people, "Nil" or "NØNOS", or giving it a name or logo that could be mistaken for them.
- Using either logo in your product, website, app store listing or marketing.
- Suggesting that the NØNOS foundation made, checked or endorses your version.

If you publish a fork or host one for others, give it its own name and logo and take ours out of the interface. The AGPL still applies to your code in full: people who use your version over a network are entitled to its source.

To ask for permission, open an issue in this repository.
