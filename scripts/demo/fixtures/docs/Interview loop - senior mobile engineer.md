# Interview loop: senior mobile engineer

Four conversations over two weeks, each one owned by a single interviewer who writes notes the same day.

## Stages

1. **Intro call** with Ada, 30 minutes: the role, the team, what they want next.
2. **Take-home review** with Cleo, 60 minutes: walk through their project together.
3. **System design** with Cleo and a guest engineer, 60 minutes: offline sync for a wallet.
4. **Values and collaboration** with Ben and Dev, 45 minutes.

## Scorecard

| Area | What good looks like |
| --- | --- |
| Mobile craft | Ships smooth, accessible UI and knows platform conventions |
| Security sense | Treats keys and secrets with care without being told |
| Communication | Explains trade-offs plainly, writes good pull requests |
| Ownership | Follows work through to users, not just to merge |
